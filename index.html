What Is This?

Daily Trading Volume Report

Maintains rolling daily snapshots for audit trail.

Weekdays 7:45 AM
🏗️

Weekly Architecture Snapshot Diff

Every Sunday at 7:00 PM: runs 15 Databricks queries capturing the full dealing execution architecture state (HedgeServer-LP map, NOP exposure, CEP boundaries, execution factors, slippage benchmarks, OMS IM settings), diffs against previous week's snapshot, generates a delta report highlighting what changed, and emails it. Keeps the dealing-execution-architecture skill up to date.

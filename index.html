12 Scheduled Jobs — Work That Happens Without You

macOS LaunchAgents run background tasks on schedule, generating reports, refreshing credentials, and syncing repositories. A self-monitoring weekly report tracks all job health.

Weekly Job Report

Every Sunday at 6:00 PM: parses all job logs from the past 7 days, generates a health dashboard HTML report, shows macOS notification, opens in browser. Self-monitoring system.

Daily Trading Volume Report

Every weekday at 8:20 AM: queries Databricks Data Lake (6 granular tables) for previous trading day volume, aggregates by HSID/strategy, instrument type, and geography, generates a rule-based narrative comparing today vs 7-day averages, renders interactive HTML with Chart.js, converts to PDF, and emails to stakeholders. Includes unique user counts, country breakdown, fill ratios, latency, and slippage metrics. On failure: macOS notification + alert email with fix instructions.

Weekdays 8:20 AM
🤖

Daily Volume Report — CMA Variant

Every weekday at 8:35 AM: hybrid local + cloud pipeline. Same 9 Databricks queries as the rule-based report, plus a dual-baseline strategy matrix (today vs same-weekday avg vs month avg). Ships JSON to a Claude Managed Agent that authors the executive narrative and ranks top-3 notable shifts with hypothesis labeling (real shift / weekday seasonality / divergence).

Weekdays 8:35 AM
💰

Monthly DOB P&L Report

1st of each month at 8:00 AM: queries Databricks OMS pricing data for all Dealing-on-Book HedgeServers (121/112/8/225), aggregates DOB P&L by asset class, volume bucket, and open/close leg, generates interactive HTML dashboard with Chart.js visualizations and business analysis, converts to PDF, and emails to stakeholders.

1st of Month 8 AM
🔗

Weekly Cross-Project Dependency Tracker

Every Sunday at 6:30 PM: searches Jira for blocked/blocking issues across DEAL, EMM, and BDDMD projects via REST API. Builds cross-project dependency graph, enriches blocked issues with last comment preview and exact blocked-since duration via changelog API. Generates HTML report with assignee heatmap, priority breakdown, week-over-week trend (new/resolved/net), and auto-comments on stale blocked tickets (14+ days). Converts to PDF and emails to stakeholder.

Sun 6:30 PM
⚡

Reforge Auth Warm-up

Every Sunday at 8:00 PM: pre-flight Reforge MCP initialize probe ~13 hours before the Monday weekly trading issues report. Catches cold-start / auth drift Sunday evening. If probe times out at 45s, auto-invokes --auth recovery (usually succeeds silently). If recovery also fails, sends an urgent action-needed email and opens the Reforge web console. Eliminates Monday-morning report failures from week-idle MCP sessions.

Sun 8:00 PM
⌨️

Swap Ctrl ⇄ Cmd

Runs at every login: applies the macOS keymap that swaps Control and Command on the external keyboard layout. Stays out of the way until rebooted, then auto-reapplies. Removes the daily friction of remembering to re-run the keymap script after restarts.
